Return on capital should count only the assets that earn today. A machine that has been under construction for three years earns nothing yet; a theme park in its ramp-up years earns less than an established one that cost the same. Set % in use below 100 for assets like these.

Some liabilities are free funding from operations. Payables are money suppliers lend the business at no cost, so netting them off shows the capital the owners actually put in. Advances from customers or debt may not deserve the same treatment. Tick Net out for each liability to subtract.

Gross assets in use = Total assets − Σ asset × (1 − % in use)

Net assets in use = Gross assets in use − Σ netted liabilities
