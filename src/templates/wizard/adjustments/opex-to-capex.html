Some expenses build long-lived assets. Buffett treats GEICO's advertising as an investment in future policyholders; a drug company's R&D builds a pipeline that earns for years. Accounting expenses these costs as they are incurred, out of conservatism. When valuing a business, you may re-classify them as capital expenditure instead.

Tick Capitalize for each expense that builds an asset and enter its useful life in years.
